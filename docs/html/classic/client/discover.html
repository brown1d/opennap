I am the World Trade Center
Look Around You
Electronic
I am the World Trade Center Website
Recording entirely on a single laptop computer, I am the World Trade Center brings organic sounds to a digital world. Marrying samples and loops from long-lost records with Amy Dyke's elegant vocals, they produce a unique and catalyzing mix, recalling artists as varied as St. Etienne, The Stone Roses, Nancy Sinatra and Fatboy Slim.
